What it is

A bilingual prompt directory for research, writing, learning, data, development, product work and everyday tasks. Templates have fillable variables, workflows explain how steps connect, and research and public-case references can be traced to their sources.

Features

  • 224 Chinese templates with a matching English catalog across 16 task categories
  • 19 workflows that connect the output of one step to the next
  • Fill template variables, copy the result and share a link to the template
  • Search by task and evidence type; download the machine-readable JSON catalog

Note

Evidence labels describe research or public cases for similar tasks. The templates themselves are written for this project.
