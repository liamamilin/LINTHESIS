Over 80 design systems from well-known products, rendered as pages you can browse, compare and take away.

About

Each design system lives in a single DESIGN.md: colors, type, radii, spacing and components. Lookbook renders the same sample page from those tokens, so you can see what Stripe, Linear or Notion feel like without reading the spec.

Features

  • Browse: grouped by category, searchable in Chinese or English, filterable by dark or light
  • Preview: every template rendered as a complete sample landing page
  • Inspect: palette, type scale, radii and spacing, and every component definition
  • Compare: put two design systems side by side, or draw two at random
  • Export: AI prompts, CSS variables, Tailwind config and more, bundled in one click
