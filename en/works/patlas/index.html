P Atlas

Possibility Library

Explore what a topic can be used to do: every possibility states its conditions and traces back to its sources.

Released Released and ready to use.

Try it online ↗GitHub ↗

What it is

It starts from achievable outcomes rather than feature lists. Each topic has a map of possibilities: what the outcome is, which conditions and components it needs, and which sources support it, all on one card. “Source-backed” does not mean tested in practice; the page states each item's evidence status, and directions still to be verified are marked separately.

Features

  • 38 topics across 11 domains, with topic research reports and source lists
  • Browse by use, filter by capability path and evidence status, or search the whole library
  • Each possibility lists the outcome, conditions and limits, required components and source passages
  • Raw entries as JSON and Markdown, plus the research validation record
