What it is

A collection of capability cards for personal operations, strategy, product, engineering, design, content, growth, operations, and legal/finance work. Each card explains when to use it, how it works, the intended output and common misuses, with links to complementary tools. Your selected toolkit stays in the local browser.

Features

  • 36 capability cards organized across nine work roles
  • Drawer and X-Ray views for thinking, memory, building, automation and distribution
  • Filter by keyword, tool format and time, quality or risk leverage
  • Cards explain triggers, mechanisms, deliverables and complementary tool chains
  • Build a personal loadout, inspect role coverage and export Markdown; switch between Chinese and English
