Glint

From scattered thoughts to structured tasks

Choose a useful thinking method where you work, and turn a vague intent into a structured task for AI.

GitHub ↗

What it is

“Help me think this through” can mean breaking down a problem, testing an assumption or comparing options. Glint retrieves cognitive cards locally, right where you work. Choose a method, add the question and constraints, then copy or insert a fillable prompt, checklist or resource. Ambient cognitive seeds introduce perspectives you have not yet thought to seek.

Features

  • A shortcut recalls three cognitive cards with prompt insertion, checklists and resource actions
  • Local hybrid retrieval and reranking draw on Cognitive Language Engineering and several taxonomy libraries
  • Browse ambient cognitive seeds, skip or hide cards, or expand them for reading
  • Fill variables in place, copy content or open it in an AI tool
  • A local console manages shelves, content supply, usage feedback and discovery settings
