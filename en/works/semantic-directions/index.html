Semantic Direction Taxonomy

Explore directions of meaning

Explore the meanings, expression forms and English equivalents of directions such as existence, change, movement and action.

Visit website ↗GitHub ↗

What it is

Organized around “expression = RU + semantic direction,” this searchable reference unfolds directions into a hierarchy. Entries connect explanations with expression forms, while the sidebar supports navigation and comparison of related meanings.

Features

  • 15 main categories, 35 subcategories and 201 semantic directions
  • 1,058 expression forms connect directions to concrete language
  • Expandable tree navigation linked to the reading pane
  • Global search, section links and mobile reading
