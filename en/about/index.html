About

Think it through, write it down, build it, and share it.

I started in data science, machine learning and statistics, and wrote four books on R and statistical analysis. What interests me now is AI, blockchain, software development, business and entrepreneurship. The subject has changed; the way I work has not: understand the problem first, explain it with words and with things that work, then put it out in the open.
