About

I spent years in data analysis. Now I spend more of my time on AI and software.

Earlier on, my practice and writing centered on data science, machine learning and statistics. I wrote four books on R and statistical analysis, and turned Python, data warehousing, A/B testing and metric systems into online guides.

What interests me now is AI, blockchain, software development, business and entrepreneurship. They share one thing: each turns an idea into something that runs and gets used. So I started building software myself, and wrote up what I learned from AI coding tools as Mastering Codex and Mastering Claude.
